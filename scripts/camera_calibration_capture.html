Doggo Checkerboard Capture

Use this page to save synchronized left, forward, and right checkerboard snapshots into the repo. After you collect a good spread of captures, solve the calibration with the Python helper.

Waiting for camera access. Start the three streams, hold the checkerboard where all three cameras can see it, and then save several captures from different positions.
Friendly names and device choices are stored in this browser so the three identical USB cameras stay easier to track.

Capture Tips

  • Use a printed rectangular checkerboard and keep it upright while moving it around.
  • Collect 12 to 20 captures with the board centered, near the edges, tilted, and at different distances.
  • For left calibration, the full board must be visible in left plus forward. For right calibration, it must be visible in right plus forward.
  • After capture, run `./.venv/bin/python scripts/camera_calibrate_checkerboard.py --capture-dir config/calibration/captures/<session>`.
